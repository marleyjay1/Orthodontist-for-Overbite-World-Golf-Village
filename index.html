What Is an Overbite?

An overbite describes the vertical overlap of the upper front teeth over the lower front teeth. In some patients, the overlap is more pronounced and can contribute to orthodontic problems.

A significant overbite may be associated with:

  • Difficulty biting or chewing comfortably
  • Excessive tooth wear
  • Irritation of the gums or roof of the mouth
  • Crowded or misaligned teeth
  • Jaw discomfort in some cases
  • Concerns about the appearance of the smile

Because every bite is different, an orthodontist should evaluate the teeth, jaws, and overall alignment before recommending treatment.

How Is an Overbite Evaluated?

During an orthodontic consultation, the orthodontist evaluates several aspects of your smile and bite. This may include examining:

  • Upper and lower tooth alignment
  • The amount of vertical overlap
  • Tooth crowding and spacing
  • Jaw relationship
  • Bite function
  • Facial balance
  • Overall oral health

The orthodontist can then explain your treatment options and create a plan based on your specific needs.

Braces and Clear Aligners for Overbite

There is no single treatment that works for every overbite. Braces can provide controlled tooth movement and are commonly used for a wide range of orthodontic problems.

Clear aligners may also be an option for certain patients. They use a series of removable trays to gradually move teeth toward their planned positions.

The best choice depends on the complexity of the overbite, tooth position, patient age, treatment goals, and other clinical factors. Your orthodontist can determine whether braces, clear aligners, or another orthodontic approach is appropriate.
